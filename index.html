      A New Chapter

      not the end of our story, but the beginning of our life

      অপেক্ষার গল্পটা শেষ হলো। এবার শুরু আমাদের সংসারের গল্প।

      For so long, we loved each other in the future tense. One day. After the exams. After graduation. When we're finally together. I counted days without knowing where the counting would end, and you waited through every single one of them with a patience I'll spend my whole life trying to deserve.

      I imagined this day more times than I'll ever admit: how it would feel, whether my voice would hold, what it would be like to hear you say qubool. And then, almost quietly, it simply happened. On the 11th of September, the waiting ended.

      Something changed that day. "Us" stopped being a plan and became a fact. You are not the girl I'm waiting to marry anymore, Babui Pakhi. You are my wife. I'm not inviting you into my heart; you already live here. This is our home.

      The wedding was one day. The marriage is every day after it: slow mornings, tired evenings, small repairs, learning each other all over again. That's the part I'm most looking forward to. Let's build it gently, and let's build it well.

      Yours, in this life and the next Mehrab · your husband
